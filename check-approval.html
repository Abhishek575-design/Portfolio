Case study · SpringVerify

Improving the approval workflow for unresolved background verification checks

Company overview

SpringVerify is a background verification platform by Springworks. Its Super Admin Portal is the internal tool the ops team uses to run and resolve candidate checks.

My role

Product Designer

Research with the ops team, flows, interface design, and handoff with 1 full stack engineer and 1 QA.

Tools

Duration

Jan – Mar 2025

Team

1 Designer, 1 Full stack engineer, 1 QA

About company

SpringVerify is a background verification platform by Springworks, helping HR, compliance, and Operations teams manage candidate verification across education, employment, address, identity, and world checks.

Checks covered

Education · Employment · Address · Identity · World

Where it broke

When checks became unresolved or inconclusive, reports could be finalized before Company Admins had a chance to review or provide additional information.

Problem

Clients had no opportunity to act before unresolved verification checks were finalized.

01 · Trigger

When a verification check became UTV, Inconclusive, or Insufficient Closed, the workflow continued toward report finalization without giving the Company Admin an opportunity to review the result, provide additional information, or contest it.

02 · Knock-on

This created a reactive workflow. When new information surfaced after the report was delivered, Operations had to reopen the check, coordinate follow-ups, and run another verification attempt.

03 · Cost

The result was avoidable operational effort, delayed resolution, and friction for clients.

The problem wasn't failed verification. It was the lack of collaboration before the decision became final.
Research & discovery

Rather than immediately designing the interface, I focused on understanding how different teams interacted with unresolved verification results.

I reviewed the existing verification workflow, walked through multiple production scenarios with stakeholders, studied competitor behavior, and identified the operational bottlenecks that created unnecessary manual work.

Three users. One broken workflow.

The same unresolved check created a different problem for each role — from configuring the rules, to making the decision, to executing the re-verification.

Super Admin
Re-verification rules had to be configured at the system/package level.
There was no way to define which checks clients could contest, or who could contest them.
Grade triggers and applicable check types couldn't be set per client package.
Without an SLA or auto-close, any added review step risked stalling reports.
Company Admin
Unresolved checks could move toward report finalization without client input.
They couldn't easily provide additional information or contest the result before finalization.
By the time they saw the issue, the workflow often required another round of verification.
They were informed about the problem after the decision had effectively been made.
Operations
Ops had to reopen completed checks when new information became available.
Re-verification created additional operational work.
The workflow could involve follow-ups and manual coordination.
Every unresolved check that needed another attempt became operational cleanup.

Why the loop happens

No client visibility

Company Admins couldn't review unresolved checks before reports were generated.

Delayed information

Critical documents and details arrived after reports were finalized.

Repeated operational work

Operations had to reopen completed checks and restart verification.

Higher operational cost

Credit notes and manual interventions increased support effort.

Proposed direction

Pause verification on unresolved checks and let Company Admins review the result before it reaches the final report.

How might we give Company Admins a meaningful decision point without adding complexity for Super Admins or Operations?

The solution consists of four key components:

Set up once

Configuration

Admins set re-verification rules once, per package – which checks can be contested, and by whom.

Visibility before the report

Review queue

Every inconclusive check surfaces in one pending-review list, so nothing waits behind a finished report.

Information arrives on time

Review details

Accept the finding, or contest it with supporting documents attached in the same screen.

No new bottleneck

Automation

Reminders chase the admin; unanswered reviews auto-close on the SLA so reports never stall.

Design

01.

One toggle turns on admin review

Super Admins: Admins set re-verification rules once, per package – which checks can be contested, and by whom. Grade triggers, applicable check types and the auto-close offset sit inside the same form.

02.

One place to manage every review

Company admin: Pending requests can be filtered by verification status, searched by candidate, and sorted by response deadline. This helps Company Admins focus on the most urgent reviews while ensuring no unresolved check is missed.

03.

See unresolved checks in context

Check level information: Unresolved checks are shown directly at the individual verification-check level, making unresolved checks visible in context. Admins can review the available information and take action to accept the result or request re-verification without leaving the check.

04.

Review the full story before deciding

Company admin: See candidate details, verification history, contact attempts, and supporting documents together at the check level. Review the evidence, understand what happened, and decide whether to accept the result or request re-verification.

05.

Keeping the check in the loop

Company admin: If a verification result is incomplete or based on outdated information, Company Admins can submit additional details and supporting documents to restart the verification process.

06.

Close the loop with a clear outcome

Company admin: Accepted decisions are recorded directly on the check, showing who made the decision and when. This creates a clear audit trail and prevents Operations from reopening or reprocessing the check unnecessarily.

Outcome

The final solution introduced a configurable approval workflow that fits into the existing verification pipeline while giving Company Admins visibility before unresolved checks are finalized.
