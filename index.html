About me

Designing with a product lens

I'm a product designer who enjoys turning complex problems into simple, useful experiences.

I started with a curiosity for visual design, but over time that grew into a deeper interest in understanding why something should be designed in the first place. Today, I work across research, product strategy, interaction design, and prototyping to turn ambiguous problems into clear product experiences.

I care about the details, but I care more about whether the experience actually works. I like working closely with users, stakeholders, and engineers, challenging assumptions, exploring ideas, and staying involved from the first sketch to the shipped product.

How I work

From ambiguity to shipped product.

01

Understand the problem

Talk to users and stakeholders, understand the business constraints, and identify where the current experience breaks down.

02

Frame & prioritize

Synthesize what I learn, define the core problem, and align with stakeholders on what matters most.

03

Explore possibilities

Explore multiple approaches, challenge assumptions, and compare solutions against user needs, business goals, and technical constraints.

04

Design & validate

Build high-fidelity experiences and prototypes, review them with users and stakeholders, and iterate based on what we learn.

05

Ship & learn

Work closely with engineering through implementation, validate the shipped experience, and use outcomes to inform the next iteration.

AI in my workflow

How I use AI to move from idea to shipped product.

Compress days of competitor analysis into hours. Synthesize user feedback, spot patterns, and turn messy discovery into focused design briefs.

Run every key decision through an AI adversary. Uncover edge cases, spot flow gaps, and pressure-test solutions before stakeholders see them.

Turn wireframes into interactive prototypes with real code. Ship something testable in a day, so feedback lands earlier and ships cleaner.
